Backup & export

Your data lives only in this browser. A JSON backup is the way to keep it safe or move it — it is always included, never locked.

Restoring asks for confirmation first, then replaces everything after each record is checked. Only backups made by SoberCount are accepted, and restore is blocked while sample data is on.

How your data works

Storage: everything is saved in this browser's local storage on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account.

Backup: download a JSON backup to keep your counters safe — restoring it on any device brings everything back.

Sample data: the flask button snapshots your real data, shows a made-up example, and restores your snapshot exactly when tapped again.

Exports: backups and CSV exports are blocked while sample data is on, so the example can never be mistaken for your real records.

A note on what this app is: SoberCount is a counter and diary tool. It is not medical advice and does not provide treatment, detox, or withdrawal guidance. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US Suicide and Crisis Lifeline). For support with quitting, a doctor, counselor, or a local support group can help in ways an app cannot.

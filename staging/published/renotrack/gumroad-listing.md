# Renovation Budget Tracker & Remodel Cost Calculator

## 🎯 THE PROBLEM

Most remodels go over budget — not because the work costs more than expected, but because nobody is tracking the real numbers as they happen.

Receipts pile up in a drawer. Contractor quotes live in three different text threads. Then comes the "while you're at it…" change — a better countertop here, moving a wall there — and suddenly you're $8,000 past the plan with no clear record of where it went.

Spreadsheets don't survive the job site. They're awkward on a phone, easy to break with one wrong formula, and nobody opens them after week two.

## ✅ THE FIX

This is a private renovation budget tracker that lives in a single file on your device. Set category budgets, log every expense, compare contractor bids side by side, and log change orders with proper cost impacts — all in one place, all offline, with no account and no subscription.

Open it on your phone at the property with zero bars and it just works. Your numbers are always with you, always current, and always yours.

## ⚠️ READ FIRST

Your data is stored in your browser's localStorage **on your device only** — nothing is ever sent to a server, and there is no account to recover it from. That means:

- Your budgets, expenses, quotes, and change orders live only in the browser you use.
- Use **Export** regularly to download a JSON backup file (one click). Keep copies somewhere safe.
- If you clear your browser's site data, your unsaved work goes with it — the backup file is your safety net.
- Moving to a new phone or computer? Export on the old device, Import on the new one. Done.

## 🚀 After download

1. Unzip the file and double-click `index.html` — it opens in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) on Mac, Windows, or your phone. No install, no setup.
2. Click the flask icon to load sample data and explore, or start fresh with the **+ New** project button.
3. Set your category budgets on the Budget tab, log your first expense, and add your contractor quotes.
4. Hit **Export** once to save your first backup file.

## ✨ What it does

- **Whole-remodel dashboard** — total budget, what's spent, what's committed, what's left, and % used, on one screen.
- **13 renovation-ready budget categories** — Demolition, Structural, Plumbing, Electrical, Kitchen, Flooring, Labor, and more, each with a budgeted-vs-actual progress bar. Over-budget categories light up red before they snowball.
- **Contractor quote comparison** — store up to 4 bids per job, see the price range instantly, and mark the accepted bid. Accepted bids count as committed spend so you never double-book your budget.
- **Change-order log** — every "while you're at it…" gets a cost impact and a proposed / approved / rejected status. Approved change orders roll into your actuals automatically and draw on your contingency buffer — no more mystery overruns.
- **Contingency buffer guard** — set your contingency as a % of budget and get warned the moment change orders start eating it (amber at 75%, red when it's gone).
- **Spending pace chart** — cumulative spend over time so you can see whether you're burning budget faster than the calendar.
- **Multiple projects** — kitchen remodel, bath flip, whole-house renovation — each with its own budgets, expenses, quotes, and change orders.
- **Works fully offline** — one self-contained file. No internet required, no cloud, no monthly fee.
- **Dark mode, mobile-friendly, printable** — clean on a phone screen, easy on the eyes after hours, and prints a tidy budget summary for your records.
- **Sounds you can mute** — subtle audio feedback on actions, with a persistent mute toggle.

## 🛠️ Honest notes

- This is a single HTML file, not a cloud service — there's no sync between devices unless you move the backup file yourself.
- Budget math is straightforward arithmetic (no hidden formulas), which also means it won't estimate costs for you — you enter the numbers, it keeps them honest.
- The spending pace chart needs dated expenses to draw; undated entries are simply left out of the chart.
- Tested in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari, on desktop and mobile.

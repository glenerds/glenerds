Frequently asked questions

What is the federal 110% rule?

On interstate moves with a non-binding estimate, the mover generally can’t demand more than 110% of the estimate at delivery. The tool’s analyzer checks your quotes against it.

How do I verify a USDOT number?

The tool walks you through checking the mover’s USDOT credentials against the federal database and flags mismatches like a name that doesn’t match the record.

Does it work for local moves?

The red-flag signals and quote comparison apply to any move; the 110% rule specifically covers federally regulated interstate moves.

Articles tell you what to watch for; this scores your actual movers and your actual quotes, then hands you a printable report.

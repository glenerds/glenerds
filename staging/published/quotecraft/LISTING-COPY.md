## Description (SaveTrail format)

**QuoteCraft**

If you are unsatisfied for any reason, please contact me for a full refund.

🎯 THE PROBLEM

Scribbling estimates on paper or in a notes app makes you look unprofessional — and hand-math on discounts and tax is where costly mistakes happen. Generic invoice tools don't understand how service quotes actually work: line items, taxable vs. non-taxable, discounts, and a clear paper trail from draft to accepted.

✅ THE FIX

QuoteCraft is a quote builder made for contractors, handymen, cleaners, landscapers, and every service business that bids jobs. Build a polished, itemized estimate in minutes: add line items with quantities and unit prices, mark which are taxable, apply a percent or flat discount, set your tax rate, and get an exact-to-the-cent total with a full breakdown. Track every quote through a Draft → Sent → Accepted pipeline, brand it with your business name and tagline, and print a clean customer-facing quote.

⚠️ READ FIRST

Your quotes are stored in your own browser (localStorage) on your own device — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and there is no account or signup. That also means your data lives only in that browser: use the built-in JSON export to back up your quotes, and import it to move them to another device.

🚀 AFTER DOWNLOAD

1. Unzip the file and open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. No install, no signup, no internet needed — it runs entirely offline from the file.
3. Click the flask icon to load sample data and explore, or start your own first quote with "New quote".

✨ WHAT IT DOES

- Build itemized quotes with quantities, unit prices, and taxable flags per line
- Percent or flat-rate discounts, plus a configurable tax rate
- Exact-to-the-cent math — all amounts computed in whole cents, no floating-point drift
- Tax-after-discount rule: tax applies only to taxable lines after the discount is split proportionally
- Draft → Sent → Accepted pipeline with a visual funnel and click-to-spotlight stages
- Cost-by-line-item bar chart and grand-total donut, both clickable to spotlight segments
- Business profile (name, phone, email, tagline) that prints on every customer quote
- Print-ready customer quote view
- JSON export/import for backup and moving between devices
- Dark mode, sound effects (mutable), and sample data mode
- Works offline from a single HTML file

🛠️ HONEST NOTES

- This is a single-file web app, not a native mobile app and not cloud software — there is no syncing between devices except via the JSON export/import.
- It does not send quotes by email or text; you print or save the quote and send it however you normally reach customers.
- It is not accounting software: it builds quotes and tracks their status, but it won't do your bookkeeping or taxes.
- Sample data is clearly marked and one click restores your real data.

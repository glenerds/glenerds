Welcome to FitnessCheckIn

One place for every client's weekly check-ins: photos, measurements, macros, adherence, your notes — then comparisons, decision logs, and feedback drafts in one click.

How it works

1 · Add clients

Clients tab → New client. Name, division, start date, goal. That's the roster.

2 · Log the weekly check-in

Check-Ins tab → pick a client → New check-in. Tick off the photo protocol, enter weight and measurements, targets vs actual macros, 1–10 adherence scores, and your notes. ~2 minutes per client.

3 · Compare week over week

Compare tab → pick a client. Deltas vs the previous week, automatically.

4 · Log the decision

Decisions tab → record what you changed (macros, training, cardio…) linked to the check-in that triggered it. Your paper trail, searchable forever.

5 · Send the feedback

Draft tab → pick a client. A message draft is composed from the latest check-in and decision. Copy it into WhatsApp, DM, or email.

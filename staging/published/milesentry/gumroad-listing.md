## Description
# MileSentry: Points & Miles Expiry Tracker

*Due to the digital nature of this product, all sales are final with no refunds.*

## 🎯 THE PROBLEM
Your airline miles, hotel points, and credit card rewards are scattered across a dozen loyalty programs — and every one of them can vanish. Some expire on a fixed date. Others disappear after 18 or 24 months of inactivity. Spreadsheets get messy, and the big tracking apps make you hand over your account logins (and now charge $49.99/year just for expiry alerts). One forgotten inactivity window and thousands of points are gone.

## ✅ THE FIX
MileSentry is an offline points and miles tracker that lives on your computer. No accounts, no logins, no password sharing, no cloud. Enter your programs and balances manually, set each program's expiry rule — a fixed date or an inactivity window — and the dashboard instantly shows what expires next, bucketed into 7, 30, 60, and 90-day urgency alerts. Log an activity and the inactivity clock resets automatically.

## ⚠️ READ FIRST
MileSentry is a single-file HTML app. All your data is stored locally in your browser's storage on your own machine — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and there are no external requests. You can back everything up anytime with one-click CSV or JSON export. Because it is fully offline, balances and activity are entered manually; it does not connect to airline or hotel websites.

## 🚀 After download
1. Download the ZIP and open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Tap the flask icon to load sample data and explore the dashboard.
3. Add your own loyalty programs: airline miles, hotel points, credit card rewards — any program with a balance.
4. Set each expiry rule, log your activity, and watch the urgency dashboard keep your points safe.

## ✨ What it does
- **Program register** — track airline miles, hotel points, credit card rewards, and other programs in one place, with balances and optional member numbers
- **Two expiry rule types** — fixed expiration dates OR inactivity windows (e.g. 24 months since last qualifying activity)
- **Urgency dashboard** — total points, points at risk within 30 days, expired count, and a "what expires next" list sorted by days left
- **8 urgency buckets** — expired, ≤7 days, 8–30, 31–60, 61–90, safe, never expires, unknown
- **Activity log** — earn/redeem/transfer entries that reset the inactivity clock and adjust balances
- **Charts tab** — points by program donut, days-remaining bars, points by urgency bucket, net activity by month; every segment is clickable with a persistent spotlight
- **Printable points-at-risk report** — a clean report sorted by urgency, ready to print
- **CSV & JSON backup** — export everything, import it back with field-by-field validation
- **Sample data** — one tap fills realistic demo programs so you can try every feature
- **Dark mode, sounds, and full offline operation** — no internet needed after download

## 🛠️ Honest notes
MileSentry does not sync with airline, hotel, or credit card websites — everything is entered manually, by design, so your credentials never leave your hands. Points have no cash value in the app; it tracks balances and expiry dates only. The app is one HTML file, so it works in any modern browser but has no mobile app version.

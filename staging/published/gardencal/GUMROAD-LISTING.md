# GardenCal

If you are unsatisfied for any reason, please contact me for a full refund.

🎯 THE PROBLEM
Planting at the wrong time is the #1 reason vegetable gardens fail. Seed packets give vague advice ("sow after last frost") but never tell you YOUR dates — and every USDA zone has different frost dates, so generic calendars don't apply to your garden.

✅ THE FIX
GardenCal calculates exact seed-starting, transplant, and harvest dates for 36 common vegetable crops from YOUR zone's average last spring frost and first fall frost dates. Pick your USDA zone (3–9), optionally enter your own custom frost dates, and get a full-year plan: month-by-month task lists, a per-crop schedule with real dates, and a growing-season gauge showing where you are in the season right now.

⚠️ READ FIRST
Your data is stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device — no account, no cloud, nothing leaves your computer. Clearing your browser data will erase your settings, so keep a backup of any custom dates you enter.

🚀 After download
1. Download and unzip the file
2. Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
3. Pick your USDA hardiness zone
4. Optionally enter your own frost dates for more accuracy
5. Follow your personalized planting calendar all season

✨ What it does
- Planting dates for 36 common vegetables, calculated from your frost dates
- USDA zones 3–9 with long-term average frost dates built in
- Month-by-month view: every task for every month, at a glance
- Crop schedule view: start-indoors, transplant, direct-sow, and harvest dates per crop
- Growing-season gauge: see what day of the season you're on, click for days remaining
- Year-at-a-glance chart: tasks per month, click a column to spotlight that month
- Custom frost date override for your exact location
- Dark mode, sound toggle, and sample data to explore before entering your own
- Works 100% offline — no account, no subscription, no ads, no tracking

🛠️ Honest notes
- Zone frost dates are long-term averages. For the most accurate dates for your town, check your local cooperative extension and enter them as custom dates.
- Harvest dates are estimates — weather and variety shift real harvests by days or weeks.
- This is a single HTML file. It runs in your browser; there is nothing to install.

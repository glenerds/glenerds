WorthAudit — Offline Net Worth Tracker
=========================================

SETUP
-----
1. Unzip this package anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click worthaudit.html to open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Bookmark it — that bookmark IS the app. No internet needed, ever.

WHAT IT DOES
------------
- Accounts: add, edit, archive, and delete asset and liability accounts
  (cash, savings, investments, real estate, vehicle, crypto, loans, mortgage, credit cards).
- Dashboard: net worth, total assets, total liabilities, monthly change,
  an asset-allocation donut chart, and your current month's cash-flow summary.
- Snapshots: record one snapshot per month and watch your net-worth trend line grow.
- Cash Flow tab: track monthly income and expenses (manual entry, no bank connections).
- Data tab: export/import JSON backups, load sample data, reset everything.

⚠️ READ FIRST — WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
--------------------------------------
WorthAudit stores everything in your browser on THIS DEVICE ONLY
(browser localStorage). It does not sync, it does not upload anything,
and it does not back itself up.

- Clearing your browser's site data / history WILL ERASE your data.
- Using private/incognito mode WILL NOT keep your data between sessions.
- Uninstalling or switching browsers loses your data.

BACKUP INSTRUCTIONS
-------------------
1. Open the Data tab in WorthAudit.
2. Click "Export JSON" and save the file somewhere safe
   (cloud drive, USB stick, email it to yourself).
3. Do this regularly — monthly at minimum, or whenever you make big changes.
4. To restore: Data tab → "Import JSON" → choose your backup file.

IMPORTANT NOTES
---------------
- Balances are manual — WorthAudit has no bank connections and no live prices.
  Update your balances yourself whenever they change.
- WorthAudit is a tracking tool, not financial advice.
- Works from file://, zero network requests, zero dependencies, zero tracking.
- Dark mode: use the moon/sun button in the header; your choice is remembered.
  The header also has a flask button that loads reversible sample data
  (tap again to restore your own data) and a speaker button to mute sounds.

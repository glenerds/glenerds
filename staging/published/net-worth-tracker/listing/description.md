🎯 THE PROBLEM
You kind of know your net worth — but "kind of" doesn't tell you whether you're actually getting ahead. Bank apps show balances, not the full picture: assets minus debts, tracked month after month, with the trend line to prove your progress.

✅ THE FIX
WorthAudit is your personal net-worth dashboard. List every asset and debt, see your net worth instantly, record a monthly snapshot, and watch your trend line grow. A cash-flow tab tracks income and expenses so you can see exactly where the money goes.

⚠️ READ FIRST
Your data is saved in your own browser only — nothing is uploaded anywhere. One HTML file; your financial numbers never leave your device. No account, no subscription — download it once, use it for every month.

🚀 AFTER DOWNLOAD
1. Open worthaudit.html in any browser — no install, no account.
2. Add your asset and liability accounts (or tap the flask for sample data).
3. Record a monthly snapshot and watch your net-worth trend line.
4. Track income and expenses on the Cash Flow tab.

✨ WHAT IT DOES
- Asset and liability accounts: cash, savings, investments, real estate, vehicle, crypto, loans, mortgage, credit cards
- Dashboard: net worth, total assets, total liabilities, monthly change, asset-allocation donut, cash-flow summary
- Monthly snapshots with a net-worth trend-line chart
- Cash Flow tab: monthly income and expense tracking (manual entry, no bank connections)
- Add, edit, archive, and delete accounts — full control of your data
- JSON export/import for backups — your records, your files
- Sample data with one-tap restore of your own numbers
- Full dark mode and optional sound effects

🛠️ HONEST NOTES
A tracking tool, not financial advice. Balances are manual — WorthAudit has no bank connections or live prices, so update your numbers yourself. Clearing your browser's site data will erase your data; export JSON backups regularly.

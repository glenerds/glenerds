🎯 THE PROBLEM
The big disc golf app keeps your own history on a leash. On a free account you can only look back at your most recent rounds — your lifetime scorecards and stats sit behind the paid Pro subscription. Players have been saying it for years: cancel Pro and your whole history disappears from view. You shouldn't have to rent your own scores forever just to see how you've played.

✅ THE FIX
BasketLog is a disc golf round log and lifetime stats tracker that runs from one single HTML file, on your device, with no account and no subscription. Log every round hole by hole for any course and layout, and your history stays yours: lifetime rounds and holes, average vs par, best rounds, a scoring-mix donut of every ace, birdie, par and bogey you've thrown, a score-trend chart, per-course averages, and a disc bag inventory with flight numbers. Already have rounds in UDisc? Export them to CSV there and import the file here — your past rounds land in your own log.

⚠️ READ FIRST (data storage)
BasketLog stores your rounds in your browser's localStorage — on this device, in this browser. It does not sync between devices, and clearing your browser's site data erases the saved log. Use the Backup button (Rounds tab) now and then: it downloads one small file with all your rounds and discs, and Restore brings them back on any device. The file you download from Gumroad is the app itself, not a backup of your data — keep both safe.

🚀 After download
1. Download the file and open it in any modern browser — no install, no internet needed after that.
2. Tap the flask icon up top to explore with the built-in sample history (your real log returns untouched when you tap it again).
3. Bringing your history with you? In UDisc, export your rounds to CSV, then here open the Rounds tab and tap "Import UDisc CSV."
4. Log your next round from the "Add a round" tab — type any course and layout name, set the pars, tap in your throws.
5. Add your discs in the Disc bag tab so your bag and flight numbers live next to your stats.

✨ What it does
- Unlimited hole-by-hole round logging for any course or layout (typed names — no course database, works anywhere)
- Lifetime stats tiles: total rounds, holes logged, average vs par, best round, birdies-or-better, aces
- Scoring-mix donut (aces, eagles, birdies, pars, bogeys, doubles and worse) — tap any slice to pin its numbers
- Score-trend chart across your whole history — tap any dot to see that round
- Best-rounds list and per-course averages with one-tap filtering of your rounds
- Full scorecard view for every round you tap
- UDisc CSV import (with duplicate protection) and full CSV export of your history
- One-file Backup / Restore for moving devices or keeping a safe copy
- Disc bag inventory with speed / glide / turn / fade flight numbers and in-bag tracking
- Printable lifetime stats report
- Dark mode, sound effects with mute, and sample data to explore first

🛠️ Honest notes
This is not a UDisc replacement: there is no course directory, no hole maps or GPS distances, no leagues or live group scoring — those need their network, and this file never phones home. It does one job: your personal round history and stats, kept by you, with no subscription. Stats come only from rounds you enter or import; there are no official ratings calculations. "UDisc" is named only because its CSV export imports here; this product is not affiliated with UDisc.

📦 What's included
One HTML file (the complete app). Open it and it runs — on Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook, Android or iPhone browsers, online or off.

How your data works

Storage: entries are saved in this browser’s local storage on this device only.

Restore: restoring a backup asks for your confirmation first, then replaces the complete diary after each record is checked. Records without a valid date or ID are skipped; duplicate IDs are dropped. Restore is blocked while sample data is on, and only backups made by this app are accepted.

Sample data: the flask snapshots your real diary, shows a made-up diary, and restores your snapshot exactly when tapped again.

Exports: exports and backups are blocked while sample data is on, so a made-up diary cannot be mistaken for your real records.

**HenTrack**

🎯 THE PROBLEM
You love your backyard flock, but the numbers live in your head — or on scattered scraps of paper. How many eggs did the girls lay this month? What are you actually spending on feed and bedding? Are those dozen eggs you sold covering their cost, or are you losing money on every carton?

✅ THE FIX
HenTrack is an offline egg and flock tracker that turns your daily counts and receipts into real answers: lay rate %, true cost per dozen, and profit vs. spend on every egg you sell.

⚠️ READ FIRST
Your data is stored in your own browser on your own device — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and there is no account. If you clear your browser data, your records go with it, so use the built-in JSON export to back up any time.

🚀 After download
1. Open the ZIP and double-click index.html — it opens right in your browser.
2. Tap the flask to load sample data and explore, or start logging your own flock.
3. Log eggs daily, and add feed and supply costs as they happen.

✨ What it does
- Daily egg log — counts, damaged eggs, notes (double yolks, first eggs, molting dips)
- Flock records — names, breeds, hatch dates, laying/molting/broody status
- Feed & cost tracker — feed, bedding, supplements, vet, equipment, categorized
- Automatic math — lay rate %, cost per dozen (trailing 30 days), 14-day egg chart
- Egg sales log — dozens sold, revenue, all-time profit vs. spend
- Dark mode, works on phone, tablet, and computer

🛠️ Honest notes
- Built as a single HTML file — no installer, no internet needed after download.
- Sample data is included so you can try every feature before entering your own flock.
- Cost-per-dozen math uses your trailing 30 days of eggs and costs.

📦 What's included
- HenTrack app (single HTML file, works offline)
- The gallery shows the real app with sample data

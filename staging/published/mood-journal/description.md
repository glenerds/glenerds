🎯 THE PROBLEM

Popular mood apps lock the good stuff behind a subscription: the pattern stats, the unlimited moods and goals, the full history and exports. One well-known mood app charges $35.99 a year for features that used to be a one-time purchase — and longtime users have been vocal about lifetime purchases quietly turning into subscriptions. If you just want to track how you feel and see what actually lifts your days, you should not have to rent your own journal back month after month.

✅ THE FIX

BrightDays is a mood tracker and journal that runs entirely on your device, in one downloadable file. Log a mood in seconds, tag the day's activities, and BrightDays shows you the patterns: which activities go with your better days, your monthly trend, your weekday averages, and a full year-in-pixels calendar. Everything the subscription apps charge for is simply included: unlimited custom moods, unlimited activities and goals, advanced stats, CSV and JSON export/import, and a printable monthly report. No account, no cloud, no subscription — your data never leaves your browser.

⚠️ READ FIRST (data storage)

BrightDays stores everything in your browser's local storage, on this device only. That means: no sync between phone and computer, no automatic backup, and clearing your browser data erases the journal — export a JSON backup now and then (one click, in the app). The optional passcode is a simple local lock for casual privacy on a shared device; it is not encryption. BrightDays is a personal journal, not a medical or therapy tool.

🚀 After download

2. Open the file in any modern browser — phone, tablet, or computer. It works fully offline.
3. Tap a mood to log your first day (about ten seconds), or press the flask button (top right) to explore with sample data first — your real journal stays saved and comes back untouched.
No install, no account, nothing else to set up.

## Description body

Daily Habit Tracker

If you are unsatisfied for any reason, please contact me for a full refund.

🎯 THE PROBLEM
Most habit trackers make you create an account, sync your personal routines to their servers, and pay a monthly subscription to unlock basic features like streaks or statistics. Your habits — some of the most personal data you have — end up on someone else's computer.

✅ THE FIX
A daily habit tracker that lives entirely in one file on your device. No account. No sign-up. No subscription. No servers. Open the file, tap your habits each day, and watch your streaks grow — with heatmaps, completion rings, and insights that show your progress at a glance.

⚠️ READ FIRST
Your data is stored only in your browser on your own device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent anywhere — which also means your habits won't transfer between devices unless you move the backup file yourself. Use Export regularly to keep a backup.

🚀 AFTER DOWNLOAD
1. Unzip the file and open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Tap the flask icon to load sample data and explore, or tap "+ New habit" to add your first real habit.
3. Check in each day by tapping a habit — streaks, heatmaps, and insights update automatically.
4. Use Export to save a backup file anytime.

✨ WHAT IT DOES
- One-tap daily check-ins with streak and longest-streak counters
- GitHub-style activity heatmap for every habit — your whole year at a glance
- Rest days that pause your streak without breaking it
- Partial credit for half-done days (optional per habit)
- Daily or weekly schedules (e.g. gym on Mon/Wed/Fri)
- Per-day editor: fix a missed check-in and add notes retroactively
- Completion rings and weekly check-in mix charts
- Dark mode with system preference detection, persisted
- Sample data mode — try everything before adding real habits
- Export / Import (JSON + CSV) for backups and moving between devices
- Works fully offline — no internet needed after download
- Sound effects with mute toggle (WebAudio, no audio files)

🛠️ HONEST NOTES
- One single HTML file — no installer, no app store, nothing to update.
- Data lives in your browser's localStorage on one device; clearing browser data without a backup will erase your habits.
- Unlimited habits, on any device with a modern browser.

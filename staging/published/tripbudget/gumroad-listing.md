## 🎯 THE PROBLEM
Stop guessing what your vacation will cost — and stop coming home to credit-card shock.

Most travel expense apps only track spending *during* the trip, leaving you completely in the dark while you're booking flights and hotels. Spreadsheets break on mobile, need constant internet, and put your financial data on someone else's cloud. Without a clear per-day allowance and a concrete savings plan before you leave, it's far too easy to overspend and only find out when the statement arrives.

## ✅ THE FIX
A self-contained trip budget planner and travel expense tracker that starts working *before* you book anything. Set your total budget, split it across flights, lodging, food, transport, activities, shopping, and insurance, and instantly see your precise per-day allowance. The built-in savings target calculator tells you exactly how much to save each week to arrive fully funded. On the road, log expenses offline in any local currency — the app converts them at the exact exchange rate you enter. No roaming, no cell service, no subscription.

## ⚠️ READ FIRST (Data Storage)
- **Everything stays on your device:** the app runs from a single HTML file and saves all trips, budgets, and expenses to your browser's local storage. No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
- **Back up your data:** use the built-in JSON export/import to back up or move your data between devices. If you clear your browser data without exporting first, your saved trips are gone — keep a backup.
- **Import replaces everything:** importing a backup file replaces all trips currently in the app (you'll be asked to confirm first).

## 🚀 After download
1. Download `index.html` from your Gumroad library.
2. Double-click `index.html` — it opens in any modern browser on your phone, tablet, or computer. No install, no sign-up.
3. Create your first trip, set your dates and budget, and start planning.

## ✨ What it does
- **Pre-trip budget allocation** — split your total budget across 8 travel categories, with automatic per-day allowance from your trip dates
- **Savings target calculator** — enter your departure date and goal; get an exact per-week and per-month savings plan
- **Multi-currency expense tracking** — log spending in the local currency; converts at the exchange rate *you* enter, fully offline
- **In-trip dashboard** — total spent, remaining, % of budget used, days until departure / days left, with tap-to-expand math on every stat
- **Daily spending view** — each day's spending vs. your daily allowance, with per-day category breakdowns
- **Over-budget warnings** — progress bars and badges turn red the moment a category or day goes over
- **Multiple trips** — plan Japan in October and Paris in December side by side
- **Clickable charts** — tap any donut segment, bar, or legend item to spotlight it; tap again to release
- **Sample data mode** — one tap fills realistic demo data so you can explore; your data is restored when you toggle it off
- **Dark mode, print-friendly trip summary, JSON export/import** for backup and moving between devices

## 🛠️ Honest notes
- Exchange rates are entered manually — that's deliberate: it matches whatever cash or card rate you actually get abroad, and it keeps the app fully offline with no dependence on live rate APIs.
- This is a browser-based single file, not a native app-store app. It works anywhere a modern browser runs, including from `file://` with no server.
- Money math is exact to the cent (no floating-point drift), so your totals always reconcile.
- Built for Type-A travel planners, budget honeymooners, family vacation organizers, backpackers, and anyone who's opened a post-trip credit-card statement and winced.

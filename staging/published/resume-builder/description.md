🎯 THE PROBLEM

You need a professional resume — not another subscription to remember to cancel.

You sit down to update your work history, pick a template, and get everything looking right. Then you reach the download screen and discover the Word or PDF file costs extra — or worse, it's a "trial" that quietly turns into $25.95 every four weeks. Some builders even give you plain text for nothing but lock the formatted Word and PDF downloads behind the paywall.

And every time you apply somewhere new, you need a slightly different version: different skills up front, different wording. One resume file quickly turns into five confusing copies named final-v2-real-final.

✅ THE FIX

ResumeNest is a resume builder, resume maker, and CV maker that runs in your browser from one downloaded file. Open it, type your details, pick one of three ATS-friendly single-column templates, and download your resume as an editable Word document (.docx), a PDF through your browser's print button, or plain text for copy-paste application portals.

Tailoring for a specific job is built in: paste the job posting into the keyword checker and get a keyword-match percentage, with the missing keywords listed — tap one to add it to your skills (only if it's true for you). An 11-point ATS checklist walks the things tracking software and recruiters actually trip on. There's a cover-letter builder with a solid starter draft, and unlimited saved resume versions so each application gets its own copy.

No account. No subscription. No charge appearing at the download button.

⚠️ READ FIRST (data storage)

ResumeNest stores your resumes in your browser's local storage, on this device only. That means: no sync between phone and computer, and clearing your browser data erases what's saved — use the Backup button now and then (one click) to download a copy you can restore anywhere with Restore. The downloaded HTML file is the builder, not a backup of what you typed. This is a resume-building tool, not a writing service or a recruiter.

🚀 After download

1. Unzip the download and open resumenest-resume-builder.html in any modern browser — phone, tablet, or computer. It works fully offline.
2. Fill in your details, or tap the flask icon (top right) to explore with sample data first — your real information stays saved and comes back untouched.
3. Paste a job posting into the checker, close the keyword gaps that honestly apply, run the ATS checklist, then download Word, PDF, or plain text.

🛠️ Honest notes

• The keyword-match percentage measures overlap with the posting's words — it is not an employer's ATS score, a ranking, or a promise of interviews. Never add skills you don't have to raise it.
• The templates are deliberately single-column and text-based: that's what tracking software parses correctly. No photos, no two-column designs, no graphics.
• PDF is made through your browser's Print → Save as PDF; glance at page breaks before applying.
• Word files can render slightly differently between Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice — open yours once to check it.
• Data lives in this browser on this device; it does not follow you unless you Backup and Restore.

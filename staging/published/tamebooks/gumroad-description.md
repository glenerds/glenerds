# TameBooks

If you are unsatisfied for any reason, please contact me for a full refund.

🎯 THE PROBLEM
QuickBooks and Xero charge you every month, keep your books on their servers, and speak accountant. Spreadsheets break the moment one formula does. If you have ever thought "I'm not an accountant — I just need to know who owes me, what I owe, and whether I made money" — this is for you.

✅ THE FIX
TameBooks is a complete small-business accounting app in a single file. Open it in any browser, on any computer — no install, no account, no internet needed. Real double-entry bookkeeping runs under the hood (your accountant will approve of the books), but you never touch a debit or credit. Everything is plain English: money in, money out, who owes me, what I owe.

⚠️ READ FIRST
Your data is stored privately in your own browser — nothing is ever uploaded anywhere. Receipt photos and attachments are stored the same way, and preferences like dark mode are kept on your device. Because everything stays with you: use the built-in JSON backup regularly, and note your books don't sync between computers on their own. Works straight from file:// — just double-click the file.

🚀 AFTER DOWNLOAD
1. Save the single HTML file anywhere and double-click it — it opens in your browser.
2. Tap the flask icon to load realistic sample data and click around safely (your real books stay untouched).
3. Switch sample data off and enter your first invoice — it takes about a minute.

✨ WHAT IT DOES
- Invoices with Draft / Sent / Paid / Overdue tracking, partial payments, and overdue reminders
- Bills and bill payments — know exactly what you owe and when it's due
- Expenses with receipt photo attachments
- Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow reports — click any number to see the entries behind it
- "Who owes me" and "Who I owe" aging views, so nothing slips
- Tax summary — track sales tax collected and what you owe
- Trial Balance and full journal — proper books your accountant can work with
- Bank statement import (CSV, OFX, QIF) with matching and categorization
- Recurring invoices, budgets, inventory, mileage and time tracking, fixed assets, multiple currencies
- Multiple separate companies, each fully isolated
- JSON backup & restore, plus complete CSV export of everything
- Dark mode, keyboard accessible, works entirely offline

🛠️ HONEST NOTES
- No automatic bank feeds — you import statements yourself (CSV / OFX / QIF).
- No payroll filing, no built-in tax tables, no government e-filing — it tracks what you owe; your accountant or tax software files it.
- One person, one device at a time — no multi-user sync, no cloud backup, no online payments or client portal.
- No receipt OCR — you attach receipt photos; it doesn't read them.
- No native mobile apps — it runs in any modern browser, desktop or mobile.

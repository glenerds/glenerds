How the flags work

Breeding due soon appears from 21 days before the calculated due date.

Care overdue uses the Next due date on the newest relevant care record.

Withdrawal active remains visible until the withdrawal end date passes. Always follow the product label and your veterinarian.

Average daily gain is pounds gained per day between the first and latest recorded weights.

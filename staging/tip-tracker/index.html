Why track it nightly?

Three reasons this beats a shoebox of receipts:

Your real rate. $140 in tips sounds great until the tip-out and the slow Tuesday drag your true hourly under what the kitchen makes. This does that math every shift.

Tax time. Federal law now lets tipped workers deduct qualified tips (up to $25,000 a year, tax years 2025–2028). The IRS expects you to keep daily tip records — this is that record, with cash and card kept separate because only voluntary charged/cash tips count. This app is a logbook, not tax advice; check IRS Publication 531 and your own tax pro for your situation.

Your best shifts. Weekday patterns, best jobs, best sections — the numbers that tell you which shifts to pick up and which to give away.
